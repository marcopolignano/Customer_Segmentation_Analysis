# Excel Customer Segmentation and Marketing Strategy

This portfolio project focuses on customer segmentation, behavioral analysis, and tailored marketing strategy — all developed in Excel as part of an Imperial College certification.

The analysis is based on a simulated e-commerce dataset for a fashion startup (SmartShoe Inc.) and aims to identify key behavioral patterns to support data-driven marketing decisions.

---

## Project Goals

- Segment customers based on recency, engagement, and feedback
- Analyze spending behavior and interaction metrics
- Design tailored marketing actions for each segment
- Deliver clear business insights and a final strategic proposal

---

## Key Techniques

- Descriptive statistics (mean, median, std dev, variation)
- Pearson correlation and R² analysis
- Logical segmentation (recency and engagement rules)
- Pivot-based modeling and dynamic charting
- Insight writing and strategy matching

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis revealed that:
- Recent customers spend less but remain responsive
- High engagement correlates with higher spending
- Critical or constructive feedback signals strong involvement
- Product preferences differ across segments, suggesting bundle opportunities

Each group was linked to specific KPIs and marketing actions to drive ROI, retention, and reactivation.
